Connectors are the integration bridge between APIwiz and the external tools your organization uses, from source control and CI/CD pipelines to databases and identity providers. By configuring connectors, you bring all your tools into a single, unified workspace where they can communicate effectively.

This eliminates the need to switch between multiple interfaces and keeps your data, configurations, and workflows synchronized across your entire API ecosystem.

### Role in API lifecycle management

Connectors play a critical role at every stage of the API lifecycle, from initial design and development through to deployment, security, and retirement.
