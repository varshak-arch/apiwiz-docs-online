---
title: "Recover your account"
---

If you cannot sign in to your workspace, use the recovery options available on the **Welcome to APIwiz** page to recover your workspace ID or reset your password.

## Recover your Workspace ID

If you do not remember your workspace ID, you can recover it using the email address associated with your APIwiz account.

To recover your workspace ID, do the following:

1. On the **Welcome to APIwiz** page, select **Forgot Workspace ID**.
  ![](https://admin.apiwiz.io/api/guides/images/6a49ff2b17bd7511541f7278)
1. On the **Recover Workspace** page, in the email field, enter your registered work email address.
  ![](https://admin.apiwiz.io/api/guides/images/6a49ff5c17bd7511541f729e)
1. Select **Recover Workspace**. APIwiz sends an email containing the workspace ID associated with your account.
1. Open the email from APIwiz and note the workspace ID provided.
1. In the email, select **Login** to return to the sign-in page.
  ![](https://admin.apiwiz.io/api/guides/images/6a49ffb417bd7511541f72ea)
1. Enter your Workspace ID.

## Reset your password

If you forget your password, you can reset it using the email address associated with your APIwiz account.

To reset your password, do the following:

1. On the **Welcome to APIwiz** page, enter your workspace ID in the **Workspace ID** field and wait for it to be validated.
  ![](https://admin.apiwiz.io/api/guides/images/6a49fff717bd7511541f7304)
1. Select **Forgot Password**. APIwiz sends a password reset email to your registered email address.
1. Open the email from APIwiz and select **Reset Password**. The **Choose Password** page opens.
  ![](https://admin.apiwiz.io/api/guides/images/6a4a000d17bd7511541f7312)
1. In the **Password** field, enter a new password.
1. In the **Confirm Password** field, re-enter the same password.

1. Wait for the password strength indicator to confirm that the password meets all requirements.
1. Select **Reset Password**.

Your password is updated successfully. Return to the **Welcome to APIwiz** page and sign in using your new password.
