The APIwiz managed service gives your organization a dedicated, isolated workspace a tenant hosted and operated by APIwiz. You get a ready-to-use APIwiz environment without provisioning servers, installing platform components, or managing your own infrastructure.

### Purpose

The managed service exists to remove the operational burden of running APIwiz yourself. Instead of installing and maintaining the platform on your own servers or Kubernetes cluster, APIwiz provisions and operates a dedicated tenant for you, so your team can start building and managing APIs immediately.

### Key capabilities

- **Multi-tenancy**: APIwiz supports multiple, isolated tenants on shared infrastructure. Each tenant's workspace, data, and configuration stay separate from other tenants.
- **Fastest way to get started**: Because APIwiz hosts and manages the tenant, you skip the infrastructure setup that a self-managed deployment requires no servers to provision, no databases to configure, and no platform components to install.
- **Fully managed cloud service**: APIwiz handles provisioning, scaling, upgrades, and ongoing maintenance of your tenant, so your team can focus on building and managing APIs instead of the underlying platform.

An APIwiz tenant gives your organization a dedicated, isolated workspace within the APIwiz platform. APIwiz hosts and manages the tenant for you, so you don't need to provision your own infrastructure.
