title: "Sign Up and Create Workspace"
---

APIwiz is an API lifecycle management platform that helps you design, secure, test, govern, deploy, observe, and monetize APIs from a centralized workspace.

This guide walks you through creating your first APIwiz account, verifying your email address, completing workspace registration, and signing in to your workspace.

### Prerequisites

Before creating an account, ensure that you have:

- A valid work email address
- Access to your email inbox to verify your account
- A supported web browser
- An internet connection

### Create your APIwiz account

To create a new APIwiz account, do the following:

1. Open the [APIwiz](https://%3Chttps://www.apiwiz.io/%3E) website.
  ![](https://admin.apiwiz.io/api/guides/images/6a49f49f17bd7511541f6f8b)
1. In the upper-right corner of the page, select **Log in**. The sign-in page opens where you can enter your workspace name.
  ![](https://admin.apiwiz.io/api/guides/images/6a49f5d317bd7511541f6fff)
1. If you are a new user, in the upper-right corner of the sign-in page, select **Create Account**. The **Create Your Account** page opens.
  ![](https://admin.apiwiz.io/api/guides/images/6a49f5b517bd7511541f6fe3)
1. In the email field, enter your work email address.
1. Select **Continue**.

APIwiz validates your email address and sends a verification email to the specified inbox. The page displays a confirmation message indicating that the verification email has been sent.

#### Verify your email address

1. Open the verification email sent by APIwiz.
  ![](https://admin.apiwiz.io/api/guides/images/6a49f67517bd7511541f7035)
1. Select **Verify Email**.

The verification link is required to activate your account. You must verify your email before you can continue with workspace registration.

APIwiz redirects you to the workspace registration process.

### Complete workspace registration

After verifying your email address, complete the workspace registration in two steps.

#### Step 1: Provide user information

Enter the following details:

Select **Continue**.

The system validates the information and proceeds to the next step.

#### Step 2: Create a password

After completing the user information, the **Choose Password** page opens.

To create a password, do the following:

1. In the **Password** field, enter a password for your account.
1. In the **Confirm Password** field, re-enter the same password.
1. Review the password strength indicator to verify that your password meets the required criteria.
1. Select **Create Account**.

As you enter your password, APIwiz validates each requirement and updates the password strength indicator. When all requirements are satisfied and both password fields match, the **Create Account** button becomes available.

Your APIwiz account is created successfully.

### Sign in to your workspace

After your account is created, the **Welcome to APIwiz** page opens.

To sign in to your workspace, do the following:

1. In the **Workspace ID** field, enter your workspace identifier.
  The system validates the workspace. If the workspace ID is valid, the **Username** and **Password** fields become available.

  ![](https://admin.apiwiz.io/api/guides/images/6a49fabe17bd7511541f71c3)
1. In the **Username** field, enter your username.
1. In the **Password** field, enter your password.

1. Select **Log in**.

APIwiz authenticates your account and opens the workspace dashboard. You can begin using the modules available in your workspace based on your subscription plan.

### Explore your workspace

After you sign in, the workspace dashboard displays the modules available for your plan.

![](https://admin.apiwiz.io/api/guides/images/6a49fb1717bd7511541f71d1)

With the free plan, you have limited access to the following modules:

- API Design
- API Linting
- API Sandbox
- API Testing
- API Catalog
- Registry
- Connectors

### Upgrade your subscription to APIwiz Pro

APIwiz Pro extends the APIwiz platform with advanced capabilities for enterprise-grade governance, security, observability, operational visibility, and monetization.

You can deploy APIwiz Pro on-premises on virtual machines \(VMs\), in cloud environments, or on Kubernetes clusters. You can also deploy it through the [AWS Marketplace](https://signin.aws.amazon.com/signin?redirect_uri=https%3A%2F%2Faws.amazon.com%2Fmarketplace%2Fpp%2Fprodview-umh2sxat6zaba%3F%26isauthcode%3Dtrue&client_id=arn%3Aaws%3Aiam%3A%3A015428540659%3Auser%2Fawsmp-contessa&forceMobileApp=0&oauth_region=us-east-1) by signing in with your AWS account. These deployment options give your organization the flexibility to deploy and manage APIs in the environment that best fits your needs.

To upgrade your workspace to APIwiz Pro, do the following:

1. Send an email to [info@apiwiz.com](mailto:info@apiwiz.com) or visit the [Contact Us](https://www.apiwiz.io/contact-us) page.
1. Discuss your business and subscription requirements.
1. After the commercial agreement is finalized, the request is reviewed for approval.
1. Once approved, the APIwiz Development or Operations team updates the workspace subscription.

Your workspace has been upgraded to APIwiz Pro, and the additional modules and features are now available.
