## Connecting to Apigee

Before using the gateway, you must first connect to it.

1. Log in to your APIwiz workspace and open the **API Gateways** module. On the Apigee card, select **Connect**.
1. This takes you to the **Connectors** module, where you complete the connection to your Apigee workspace.
1. Once connected, the Apigee card shows **View Details** instead of Connect.
1. Select **View Details** to open the gateway.

The gateway opens with four tabs: **Proxy**, **Policy**, **Insights**, and **Manage**. By default, the Proxy tab is selected.
