Consent Management allows your organization to collect and manage user consent for accessing specific data. Think of it like the permission screen you see when you sign in to an application using Google; where it lists the data items it wants to access \(such as your name, date of birth, or phone number\) and you choose to allow or deny.

In APIwiz, Consent Management enables your clients to define what data they want to collect from their users, configure the consent structure, and audit the data collected; all from one centralized module.

The Consent Management module includes two main sections:

- **Consent Groups**: Where you organize and configure the consents you want to collect.
- **Audit Logs**: Where you view and track the data users have submitted.
