This quickstart walks you through the end-to-end process of creating, editing, validating, and managing an AsyncAPI in Design Studio.

1. [Create an AsyncAPI](https://docs.apiwiz.io/guides?guideId=6a22b872070047a3a81f4972&groupId=Working+with+Async+API&pageId=6a3138452b6d03e0ddd4e5e3)**: **Select **\+**, then choose a creation method: **New API** to create from scratch or from a sample template, **Import** to upload an existing AsyncAPI specification file, or **Git Sync** to sync the specification from a source control repository.
1. [Open the AsyncAPI editor](https://docs.apiwiz.io/guides?guideId=6a22b872070047a3a81f4972&groupId=Working+with+Async+API&pageId=6a313b9e2b6d03e0ddd4e675)**: **In the **General Information** section, select **Edit** to open the AsyncAPI editor, then define servers, channels, messages, and schemas.
1. [Validate the specification](https://docs.apiwiz.io/guides?guideId=6a22b872070047a3a81f4972&groupId=Working+with+Async+API&pageId=6a313b9e2b6d03e0ddd4e675#c7c20d70-635b-4c98-b361-330b4639620f)**: **Review the **Violations Summary** panel and resolve any errors, warnings, or informational messages to ensure the specification is valid.
1. [Preview the API documentation](https://docs.apiwiz.io/guides?guideId=6a22b872070047a3a81f4972&groupId=Working+with+Async+API&pageId=6a313b9e2b6d03e0ddd4e675#f641cc6d-dc78-43d4-bf6e-f1d8cb6376f9)**: **Use the **Preview** panel to view the rendered documentation of the AsyncAPI, including servers, operations, messages, and schemas.
1. [Save the AsyncAPI](https://docs.apiwiz.io/guides?guideId=6a22b872070047a3a81f4972&groupId=Working+with+Async+API&pageId=6a313b9e2b6d03e0ddd4e675#0a3120e9-cee9-4331-b762-46d9a80c0bc5)**: **Select **Save** in the top-right corner to store your changes.
1. [Update the AsyncAPI status](https://docs.apiwiz.io/guides?guideId=6a22b872070047a3a81f4972&groupId=Working+with+Async+API&pageId=6a313ba02b6d03e0ddd4e67f#a2d981a0-ab99-4dac-922d-eb878d8b3568)**: **Select **Change Status**, choose the required lifecycle stage, provide a commit message, then select **Save**.
1. [Review the changelog](https://docs.apiwiz.io/guides?guideId=6a22b872070047a3a81f4972&groupId=Working+with+Async+API&pageId=6a313ba02b6d03e0ddd4e67f#6c291f88-2c60-44b0-9a70-c8e10ae98e2e)**: **Select **Changelog** to view the history of status updates, including timestamps and user details.
