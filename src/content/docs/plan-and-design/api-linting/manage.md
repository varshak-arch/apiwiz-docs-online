You can manage categories, rulesets, and rules from their respective sections in the left panel. You can also filter the reports to narrow the displayed results.

# Managing a category

To manage a category:

1. In the **Category** section, select the category you want to manage.
1. Select **Edit** or **Delete**:
  - **Edit**: Updates the category details.
  - **Delete**: Removes the category.

# Managing a ruleset

To manage a ruleset:

1. In the **Ruleset** section, select the ruleset you want to manage.
1. Select the **Edit** icon next to the ruleset name to update its details.
  Use **Edit** to update the ruleset name, description, category, or global ruleset setting. After saving, the ruleset remains under the appropriate category and ruleset list based on its configuration.
1. To delete a ruleset, go to the ruleset section and select the ruleset you want to delete. Select **Delete** from the ruleset list.
  Use **Delete** to permanently remove a ruleset.

# Filtering reports

Use the **Reports** section to narrow the displayed results. You can filter reports in three ways.

To filter reports:

1. Select the **Errors**, **Warnings**, or **Info **checkbox to filter results by severity.
1. Use the date range selector to filter results by time period.
1. Select a **Category** or **Ruleset** to narrow the results.
